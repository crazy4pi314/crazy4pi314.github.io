## Experience

I got my PhD in Physics (Quantum Computing) by starting plasma fires with lasers to hack quantum crypto devices and trying to send and detect single photons in space ([read the thesis](http://hdl.handle.net/10012/10725)). As a developer, Python is my go-to language for most things, but I also dabble in Rust and program quantum computers in Q#. In 2021 I was awarded a Python Software Foundation Fellowship, and I currently work as a developer advocate for the Python OSS community.

- Low noise optical systems on satellites
- Virtual reality hardware

- Software tools and languages for quantum computers

## Passions

I am a vocal activist for diversity and inclusion in STEM fields and often give [presentations](/events/) on my experiences and perspective as a bisexual woman in tech. Live streaming quantum software development on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/crazy4pi314) has been another great way I connect with folks to make quantum tech more accessible.
