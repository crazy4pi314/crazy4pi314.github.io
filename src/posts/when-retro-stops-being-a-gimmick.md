The trick with retro web design is not to recreate every flashing banner and beveled button. It is to remember **why** those pages felt alive.

For this theme, that means:

## Keep the nostalgia structural

Window chrome, badge walls, and status strips create atmosphere. The actual reading surface still needs to be calm.

## Let accessibility be part of the style

Visible focus rings, roomy line-height, and strong color contrast are not “modern compromises.” They are what let the weird parts stay charming instead of exhausting.

> If the visual joke makes the page harder to use, the joke has overstayed its welcome.

## Make customization obvious

If somebody opens this starter and can instantly find the site data, post content, and panel components, the design becomes an invitation instead of a trap.
